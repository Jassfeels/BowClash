Stickman Archer: BowClash: Delete your data

Developer: Smart Solutions (Sell Smartly), publisher of Stickman Archer: BowClash on Google Play.

BowClash has no accounts and never asks for your name or email. The only data that leaves your phone is anonymous play statistics (when "Share stats" is on). This page explains how to stop it and how to have it deleted.

Stop sending statistics

  1. Open BowClash and tap Settings (gear icon).
  2. Turn Share stats off. From then on, nothing is sent.

Delete statistics already sent

  1. Send an email to the developer contact address shown on the BowClash Google Play page (section "App support").
  2. Subject: BowClash: delete my data. Please include your phone model and roughly when you played, so we can find your anonymous record.
  3. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Everything we hold for your install: its random player number, level, stars, coins, diamonds, best Endless score, wins/losses, play dates, app version, phone model, Android version and region setting, and the list of game events (game opened, level won/lost).
  • Nothing is kept afterwards. We do not keep backups of deleted records beyond 7 days.

Data on your phone

Your game progress is stored only on your phone. Uninstalling BowClash, or clearing its data in Android Settings → Apps → BowClash → Storage, deletes it.
