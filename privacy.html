BowClash Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

BowClash is a stickman archery game. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run and improve the game.

What we collect

  • Game progress (levels, stars, coins, diamonds, items, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • Anonymous play statistics: when "Share stats" is on (Settings), the game sends events such as "game opened", "level won" or "level lost" with the level number to Google Analytics. Each install gets a random ID created on the device. No name, email, contacts, location or device identifiers are sent. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.
  • Advertising: if the game shows ads (for example optional "watch an ad to revive"), the ad provider (Google AdMob) may collect device information and the advertising ID as described in Google's privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for an account or any personal information.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes all progress stored on your device. Anonymous statistics are kept by Google Analytics under its standard retention and cannot be linked to you.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please use the developer contact address shown on the BowClash Google Play page.
