BowClash Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

BowClash is a stickman archery game. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run and improve the game.

What we collect

  • Game progress (levels, stars, coins, diamonds, items, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • Anonymous play statistics: when "Share stats" is on (Settings), the game sends events such as "game opened", "level won" or "level lost" to our own game server, together with a short progress summary (current level, stars, coins, diamonds, best Endless score), the app version, the phone model, the Android version and the language/region setting of the phone (for example "en_IN"). Each install gets a random ID created on the device. No name, email, contacts, precise location, advertising ID or other device identifiers are sent. We use this only to see how many people play and which levels are too hard. You can turn it off at any time in Settings.
  • Advertising: if the game shows ads (for example optional "watch an ad to revive"), the ad provider (Google AdMob) may collect device information and the advertising ID as described in Google's privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for an account or any personal information.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes all progress stored on your device. Anonymous statistics are kept on our server (hosted by Neon) for as long as the game is offered, cannot be linked to you, and are never sold or shared. To have them deleted, contact us with the request and we will remove them.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please use the developer contact address shown on the BowClash Google Play page.
